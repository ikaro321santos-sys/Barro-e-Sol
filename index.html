A workshop by the river

We started in Petrolina, between clay cracked by the sun and water that never runs dry. Every piece carries that duality: the roughness of the earth and the softness of hands that have worked it for more than twenty years.

We don't use industrial molds. Every jug, plate, or vase comes out a little different from the last — and that's exactly how we like it.

From clay to piece

1
Extraction and kneading

Clay is taken from the riverbanks and kneaded by hand until it reaches the right texture.

2
Shaping on the wheel

Each piece is raised slowly, without rushing, respecting the material's own pace.

3
Firing in a wood-burning kiln

Hours of slow fire until the clay becomes ceramic, with the natural marks only a wood kiln can give.
